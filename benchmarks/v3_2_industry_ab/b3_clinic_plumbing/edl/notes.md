## Brutally Honest Assessment of EDL v3.2 (`mep.plumbing`)

### Where EDL Helped Tremendously
1. **Zero-coordinate semantic fixture connections:** Writing `connect Exam1Basin { cold: CW_Header_North cold_dia: 15mm hot: HW_Header_North hot_dia: 15mm waste: SAN_Header_North drain_dia: 40mm slope: "1:40" vent: VENT_Header_North }` auto-resolved exact wall attachment coordinates, routed perpendicular stubs onto collector headers, assigned DFU values, calculated flow rates and supply velocities (`1.63 m/s`), and generated 59 pipes (75 segments) in ~50 lines of declarative EDL.
2. **Instant multi-artifact pipeline:** One `.edl` source compiled directly into layered AutoCAD R12 DXF (`PLUMB_COLD`, `PLUMB_HOT`, `PLUMB_DRAIN`, `PLUMB_VENT`, `PLUMB_EQUIP` cleanly overlaid on architectural layers), annotated SVG/PNG with legend/slope/DFU callouts, and an automated pipe takeoff CSV (`edl plumbing-schedule --csv`) with zero manual CAD drafting.
3. **Built-in IPC/EN 12056 verification (`P1`–`P7`):** Automatic auditing of 100% wet fixture coverage (`P1`), minimum drainage sizing/slopes (`P2`), trap-arm siphonage (`P3`), supply velocity <= 2.0 m/s (`P4`), main/heater shutoff valves (`P5`), and cumulative header DFU (`P7`) eliminated manual sizing spreadsheets.

### Where EDL Fought Me, Lacked Features, or Slowed Me Down
1. **NO built-in plumbing-vs-structural-column or plumbing-vs-dry-room (`ElecITRoom`) clash verifier:** `V7_structural_column_clearance` only checks door swings & window cutouts against columns, and `P6_cross_discipline_clearance` only checks against co-resident `building_electrical` devices (`"No co-resident electrical devices to clash with"`). EDL did **not** check whether routed plumbing pipes pierced any of the 21 structural columns or passed through `ElecITRoom`! In fact, connecting `Exam1Basin`–`Exam4Basin` directly south to the corridor headers would have silently pierced `Col_2_C`–`Col_5_C` without any EDL warning — I had to catch that manually from `arch_manifest.json` and write an external Python Liang-Barsky segment-vs-AABB clash auditor.
2. **No header-to-header T-junctions or multi-zone sanitary outfall tying:** EDL `header` only accepts node endpoints (`from: NodeA to: NodeB`), not another header, and every `soil_header` requires its own `soil_stack` endpoint. As a result, connecting `CW_Trunk_West`/`HW_Trunk_West` to the horizontal Corridor and North headers relied on geometric crossing (rather than explicit topological tee-nodes), and the 3 sanitary headers each terminate at independent soil stacks (`SoilStack_Main`, `SoilStack_Mech`, `SoilStack_North`) instead of merging into a single combined 22-DFU building drain trunk with cumulative downstream DFU propagation across tees.
3. **No dedicated `vent_end` / `vent_cap` node primitive:** To start a `vent_header`, EDL's own commercial example uses `cleanout { system: vent ... }`, which causes vent header endpoints to show up with `"COP"` cleanout labels on the SVG and requires filtering `system != 'vent'` when extracting real sanitary cleanouts for `plumbing_manifest.json`.
4. **Implicit 110mm wall-offset & 5%/95% header tap clamping:** EDL offsets wall-hosted plumbing nodes 110mm inside the host wall and clamps perpendicular header taps to `[5%, 95%]` of header length (visible in `StaffWC`, where `StaffToilet` and `StaffBasin` jog back to `X=21996`). While visually reasonable, these hidden routing rules cannot be inspected without dumping `--json`, and there is no way to place an inline valve or tee in the middle of a room/corridor (all nodes must be wall-hosted).
5. **No built-in `plumbing_manifest` or node-overlap check:** In Draft 1, accidentally placing `V_Main` and `WH1` at the exact same wall offset (`MechRoom.south at: 350mm`) produced zero EDL warnings and silently overlapped the symbols until I spotted it in the rendered PNG. Also, the SVG renderer had minor text collisions in dense areas (e.g., `COP` labels on close cleanouts, `Corridor` `±0.000` level tag near `VENT_Header_Main`, and `StaffWC` room label over fixture lines) with no label-avoidance layout.
6. **Single-file copy of B2 architecture instead of clean overlay import:** Extending `clinic_arch.edl` meant duplicating all 299 lines of B2 architecture into `clinic_plumbing.edl` rather than importing/overlaying the B2 architectural file by reference (which also risks drift, as I caught a copy-typo on `Exam2Basin` during Draft 2).
